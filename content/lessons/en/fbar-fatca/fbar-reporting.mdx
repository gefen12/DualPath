## The test

<TermChip he="דיווח על חשבונות בחו״ל" en="FBAR" />

You must file if you had a **financial interest in, or signature authority over**, accounts outside the US whose **combined value was more than $10,000 at any time** during the calendar year.

- It's the **total** that counts. Three accounts at $4,000 each, at the same moment, means you file, and you report all three.
- It's the **highest point** in the year, not the year-end balance. A salary deposit that briefly pushed you over counts.
- Money moved from one foreign account to another during the year is only counted once.

## Which accounts

Bank accounts (checking, savings, deposits), brokerage accounts, and mutual-fund style accounts. In Israel that usually means your bank account and deposits, an investment account at the bank or a broker, and in most practitioners' view, savings products such as a keren hishtalmut or kupat gemel. Joint accounts with a spouse count, and so do accounts where you only have signing rights, such as a parent's account or an employer's.

<Callout type="info">Ask your preparer how they treat your pension fund and keren hishtalmut on the FBAR. Practice varies, and it's simplest to decide once and stay consistent.</Callout>

## How to fill it in

1. For each account, find the **highest balance** during the year from your statements.
2. Convert it to dollars at the Treasury's **year-end exchange rate** for shekels, the same rate for every account.
3. File online through FinCEN's **BSA E-Filing** system. It's free, and there's no paper version.
4. Keep the statements for at least five years.

## When it's due

**April 15**, with an **automatic extension to October 15**. You don't need to ask for the extension. The FBAR goes to FinCEN, not with your tax return.

## Late or never filed?

Penalties exist, but the Supreme Court decided in *Bittner v. United States* (2023) that the penalty for a non-willful violation applies **per report, not per account**. If you missed years, look at [the Streamlined procedure](/learn/basics/streamlined-procedure) before filing old FBARs on your own.
