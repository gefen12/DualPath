## What's a PFIC?

A Passive Foreign Investment Company is US tax language for a foreign company that mostly holds investments. An Israeli mutual fund fits that description exactly — even if, inside, it simply buys the S&P 500.

## Why it hurts

- **High tax on gains:** gains can be taxed at the top US rate, plus interest — not the lower capital-gains rate.
- **No offsetting losses:** a loss on one fund can't cancel a gain on another.
- **Paperwork every year:** Form 8621 per fund — accountants charge for each one.
