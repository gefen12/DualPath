## Why bother

Your US credit history stays in the US. Israeli credit cards and loans don't appear on a US credit report. If you ever move back, rent an apartment, or take out a US mortgage, a long, clean US history makes it much easier.

## What helps

- **Keep at least one US card open,** ideally your oldest one, since length of history counts.
- **Use it now and then** and pay it in full, so the issuer doesn't close it for inactivity.
- **Pick a card with no foreign transaction fee** if you'll use it in Israel. Fees on purchases in shekels add up.
- **Pay from your US account** to avoid currency conversion on the payment.

## Cards in Israel

Israeli cards (כרטיס אשראי) usually bill once a month straight from your Israeli bank account. They're the simplest for daily life in shekels. Your Israeli and US cards do different jobs, and it's common to keep both.

<Callout type="warning">Card issuers may ask for a US address. Use your real situation and an issuer that accepts it, rather than a relative's address.</Callout>
