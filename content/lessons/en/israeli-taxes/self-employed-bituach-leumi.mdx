## Two systems, one income

<TermChip he="עצמאי" en="self-employed" /> <TermChip he="עוסק פטור / עוסק מורשה" en="small / licensed business" /> <TermChip he="ביטוח לאומי" en="National Insurance" />

When you work for yourself in Israel, as a freelancer, consultant or business owner, you deal with:

- **Israel:** income tax, VAT registration (*osek patur* or *osek murshe*), and **Bituach Leumi** (National Insurance) plus health tax on your profit.
- **The US:** income tax on the same profit, which the Foreign Tax Credit or the exclusion can usually remove, **and self-employment tax**, the US Social Security and Medicare charge on net self-employment earnings of **$400 or more**.

<Callout type="warning">Neither the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion nor the Foreign Tax Credit reduces US self-employment tax. Self-employed people in Israel are often surprised by a US bill even when their US income tax is zero.</Callout>

## Why there's no easy fix

The US has social security agreements, called "totalization agreements", with many countries that stop people paying into both systems. **Israel isn't one of them.** So a self-employed American in Israel has generally owed both US self-employment tax and Bituach Leumi on the same income.

## The 2026 Israeli change for US olim

In February 2026 the Knesset passed an amendment to the National Insurance Law that, according to published reports, exempts **new immigrants from the US** from Israeli National Insurance contributions for **five years**, on work and self-employment income for which they pay US Social Security. It applies from the start of 2026. It doesn't cover **health tax**, which is still due. The exact eligibility rules (for example, how long you've lived in Israel and what other income you have) should be checked with Bituach Leumi or an accountant.

## Salaried, with an Israeli employer?

This is mainly a self-employed problem. If you're an employee of an Israeli company, you pay Bituach Leumi through payroll and normally owe no US Social Security tax on that salary.
