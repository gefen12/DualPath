## General education, not advice

Everything on this site is general education. It is not tax, legal or investment advice, and it does not take your personal situation into account.

## Rules change

US and Israeli rules change every year. Each lesson shows when it was last reviewed and lists its official sources, so you can check what is current.

## Check your own case

Before you file, sell, open or close anything, check your own case with a qualified US–Israel tax professional.
