## What this is

DualPath is a free course for US citizens living in Israel. It explains US and Israeli taxes, bank accounts and investing in short lessons, in English and Hebrew, so you know which forms you file each year, which accounts and investments are safe, and when to get a professional.

## Who checks the content

Lessons are written from official sources, such as the IRS, FinCEN, Bituach Leumi and the Israel Tax Authority, and each one lists its sources at the end. They haven't been reviewed by a tax professional yet, so every lesson is marked as a draft until it is.

## What we collect

There are no accounts and no cookies. Your quiz answers and checklist ticks stay in your own browser. The “Ask about a product” form sends only the product name and, if you add it, your email.
