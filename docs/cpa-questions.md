# Questions for the CPA review

October 11, 2026. Shared, commentable version:
[DualPath — Questions for the CPA review](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/2f72ad81-163a-4e71-bdba-443345c0c134).
What was already checked, with sources: [content-review.md](content-review.md).

DualPath's 19 lessons, fund checker (21 products), glossary (24 terms) and 5 yearly deadlines are
written and online, but none has been reviewed by a tax professional. Every lesson shows a
"Draft — not yet reviewed by a CPA" banner until it's signed off (`cpaReviewed: true` in its
frontmatter). The questions below are where a source wasn't clear, sources disagreed, or the site
needs a professional's judgement. Start with the priority questions: they block launch.
