## Facts confirmed

- FEIE 2026: $132,900 (2025: $130,000), Rev. Proc. 2025-32.
- FBAR: more than $10,000 aggregate at any time; due April 15, automatic extension to October 15; filed with FinCEN.
- Form 8938 abroad: $200,000 year end / $300,000 any time (single); $400,000 / $600,000 (joint).
- Filing threshold 2025, single under 65: $15,750. Self-employment: $400 net earnings.
- Abroad: automatic 2-month extension to June 15; interest from April 15; Form 4868 → October 15.
- Foreign tax credit: carry back 1 year, forward 10 years. FEIE revoked → 5 years before re-electing without IRS approval.
- Child tax credit 2025: up to $2,200 per child, up to $1,700 refundable. FEIE blocks the refundable part.
- Streamlined Foreign Offshore: 3 years of returns, 6 years of FBARs, Form 14653, no offshore penalty for those meeting the non-residency test (330 days, no US abode in one of the last 3 years); otherwise Streamlined Domestic with 5%.
- *Bittner v. United States* (2023): non-willful FBAR penalty is per report, not per account.
- Form 3520: gifts/bequests from non-US persons over $100,000 in a year; penalty 5% per month up to 25%.
- US estate tax basic exclusion 2026: $15,000,000.
- Israel: no inheritance or estate tax (abolished 1981); capital gains on securities generally 25%.
- Chisachon L'Kol Yeled: ₪58/month from January 2026, parents may add ₪58; bank or kupat gemel.
- CRBA: citizen parent married to a non-citizen needs 5 years' US physical presence, 2 after age 14.
- There is **no US–Israel totalization (social security) agreement**.

## New developments added to lessons

- **IRS disaster relief for Israel** (Notice 2026-63): US federal deadlines falling up to September 30, 2027 are postponed for taxpayers whose principal residence is in Israel. FBAR relief has historically come separately from FinCEN; no 2026 FBAR notice was found. → mentioned in "Your yearly to-do list" with a "check with your CPA" note.
- **Amendment 272** (Income Tax Ordinance): reporting exemption for olim ends for residents from January 1, 2026; the 10-year tax exemption stays.
- **2026 olim tax break on Israeli work income** (Knesset, March 30, 2026): caps ₪600,000 (2026, prorated), ₪1,000,000 (2027, 2028), ₪350,000 (2029), ₪150,000 (2030). Sources disagree on the start date (November 5 vs November 25, 2025).
- **National Insurance Law amendment** (February 2026, reported as Amendment 262): new US immigrants exempt from Bituach Leumi for 5 years on income they pay US Social Security on; health tax still due.
